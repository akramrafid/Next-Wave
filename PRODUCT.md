## Product Principles

1. **Architectural Clarity over Decoration**: Structure information with crisp Swiss grids, hairlines, and typographic hierarchy straight from the Brand Guidelines—never SaaS card soup, glowing orbs, or pill-badge clutter.
2. **Evidence & Attribution Honesty**: Keep client partnerships cleanly separated from founder prior experience; never fabricate metrics, reviews, or fake clickable articles.
3. **Calm Editorial Pacing**: Balance spacious `#FFFFFF`/`#F7F7F7` Swiss paper sections against committed `#262626` Clarity Black and `#0E9ED9` → `#091345` brand gradient plates.
4. **Accessible & Tactile Craft**: WCAG AA contrast across light/dark/gradient surfaces, `prefers-reduced-motion` safety, keyboard/focus parity, and composite-only motion (`transform`, `opacity`).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast across `#FFFFFF`/`#F7F7F7` (`#262626` ink), `#262626` (`#F7F7F7`/`#FFFFFF` ink), and `#091345`/`#0E9ED9` gradient surfaces.
- Full keyboard navigation, visible `:focus-visible` outlines, Esc-to-close on menus/dialogs/chatbot, screen-reader labels, and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` support.
